## From

lit-studio (formerly journal-club), the lab's field knowledge base built on biblio's wiki tooling;
`docs/for-other-projects.md` in lit-studio. Found by the session lit-pix-ontology (2026-11-05), filed by lit-studio's
manager.

## Problem

`biblio wiki lint` hard-codes the files at the wiki root that are not pages (`schema.py`, `META_FILES`: `index.md`,
`log.md`, `README.md`, `SPEC.md`, `SCHEMA.md`, …). A project's own generated overview at the root is linted as a page.

## Evidence

lit-studio's `docs/wiki/terms.md` is generated from `docs/wiki/terms.yml` by `code/check_terms.py` (decision D38, field
term records, 2026-11-05). Lint reports on it: `FM003` "type 'meta' not in […]", plus `CIT001` noise. lit-studio worked
around it for now (commit 667b8e5b: the overview carries scope/status frontmatter).

## Request

Make the meta-file list configurable in `wiki.yml` (e.g. `meta_files: [terms.md]`), or accept `type: meta` and skip
page rules for it.

## How lit-studio will use it

`terms.md` is regenerated on every term change; with it exempt, lint on the wiki root stays clean and real findings
aren't buried.

## Tracking

lit-studio `docs/requests/registry.md`, R65; decision D38.

## Resolution (2026-11-10)

biblio 251e303 (with R50). `wiki.yml` takes `meta_files: [terms.md]`: wiki-relative paths that are not pages, added to the built-in list (`index.md`, `log.md`, `README.md`, `SCHEMA.md`, ...). Every page listing (lint, hubs, status, review, promote, embeds) uses it. The `type: meta` alternative was not added: one mechanism is enough. Test `test_meta_files_from_wiki_yml_are_not_pages`.
