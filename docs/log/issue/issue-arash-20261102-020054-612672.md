## From

## Problem

`bib/derivatives/figures/maingret_2016_HippocampocorticalCoupling/` in lit-studio holds `fig-S1..S6.{png,pdf}`, labelled as supplementary figures. They are fragments of the **main article**: S1 the title page and abstract, S2 main Fig 1 with its legend, S3 main Fig 3, S4 the reference list, S5 and S6 parts of the Online Methods. The main Figs 1-4 have no crops, and the folder has no `manifest.yml`.

## Evidence

- Found by lit-studio's blind attacker agent (`papers/maingret_2016_HippocampocorticalCoupling/breakdown-attack.md`, point A1) and checked by the session on a contact sheet of the six PNGs (2026-11-02).
- Added in lit-studio commit `425f04e4` (2026-10-24), message "supplementary figures (S1-S6) for maingret_2016_HippocampocorticalCoupling". **Origin unknown:** the missing `manifest.yml` suggests these were not made by `biblio figures run`. Please check whether `biblio figures` was run on this paper (or on its supplement PDF) and produced these.
- PDFs: published article = Zotero attachment UUG4FESD; Supplementary Text and Figures (MOESM3) = Zotero attachment BYPCANQS, both on the laptop.

## Request

1. Find out whether biblio produced these crops. If yes, fix whatever mislabels page fragments as supplementary figures.
2. Make main-figure crops (Figs 1-4) for this paper with a manifest, as for other papers.
3. If supplementary figures are in scope for `biblio figures`, crop them from the supplement PDF with their own labels (S1..S8).

## How lit-studio will use it

Breakdowns (protocol D36) do a figure pass on the key figures and link the crops from the breakdown and its interactive render; the wiki transcludes them. With no correct crops, the Maingret breakdown read the figures from page renders and links no crops.
