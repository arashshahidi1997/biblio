## Problem

Digest quotes should be the full text's own span (added 2026-10-24). Found while writing 54 wiki source pages from 53 new digests.

## Evidence

  - each agent found digest quotes marked "exact" that are not substrings of the docling text: lost double spaces (steriade_1993_NovelSlow "deep  barbiturate"), entities decoded (`&lt;` in laroche_1990, ferraris_2018), `/C24` and `/uniFB01` normalised (lee_2010), hyphens dropped or added ("reverseordered" in pfeiffer_2020);
  - the ripple-mechanisms group alone replaced 14 such quotes;
  - some digest quotes were sentence fragments (steriade_2003, kim_1995).

  Wiki lint passed the copied raw spans, so pages are fine, but every page writer has to re-copy each quote from the full text.

## Request
 when `biblio digest claims` marks a quote `exact`, store the matching span copied from `<key>.md` (with its double spaces, `&lt;`, `/C24`, run-together words), or both the clean and the raw span. Also, don't cut a quote mid-sentence where the sentence is the claim.


**Done when:** a quote copied from a digest marked `exact` is a plain substring of `<key>.md`.

## How journal-club will use it

Quotes can be copied from digests straight onto wiki pages and slides.

## Follow-up
- 2026-10-24: done (backlog D17). Each checked quote now stores `quote_raw`, the exact characters of `<key>.md` (a plain substring, `grep -F` finds it; set when it differs from the clean `quote`), `quote_line` and `quote_sentence` (the whole sentence when the quote is a fragment). `biblio digest recheck` fills these for existing digests without an LLM: on a copy of journal-club's 81 digests all 2277 exact quotes then give a plain substring.
