## Problem

The docling text of `cash_2009_HumanK` (Cash et al. 2009, Science 324:1084, "The Human K-Complex Represents an Isolated
Cortical Down-State") in the pixecog pool mixes in the neighbouring articles of the issue. The PDF is a Science reprint
that carries the end of the previous article and the start of the next one; docling converts all of it. Quote checks
and digests can match text that isn't Cash's.

## Evidence

`/storage2/arash/projects/pixecog/bib/derivatives/docling/cash_2009_HumanK/cash_2009_HumanK.md` (201 lines):

- lines 1–20: reprint cover page (licence and link text);
- about line 24: the end of the previous article ("…ficed to establish place preference in the absence of other
  reward…", a dopamine/conditioning paper), then its "References and Notes" (line 27, starting "R. A. Wise, Nat. Rev.
  Neurosci.");
- line 56: Cash et al. starts (second copy of its title heading);
- line 164: "## Crystal Structure of the Nuclear Export Receptor CRM1 in Complex with Snurportin1 and RanGTP", the next
  article.

## Request

Either trim to the article's own pages (e.g. a page range in the paper's record, or detect the title heading and the
next article's heading), or flag such files so quote checks warn. A list of other pool texts with two or more
top-level article titles would show how common this is.

## How lit-studio will use it

Quote checks against Cash 2009 (K-complex term record, D38) can be trusted without hand-checking the spans.

## Tracking

lit-studio `docs/requests/registry.md`, R69.

## Resolution (2026-11-10)

biblio 1a14bb6. Flagged, not trimmed: the texts are shared pool derivatives, and a wrong trim would lose text silently.

- `biblio fulltext KEY --span`: the article's own lines. It starts at the title heading (of several copies, the one with the most text before its references: a reprint's cover repeats the title) and ends before another article: a Title Case heading after the references that is followed by an author line. A reference list before the title is the previous article's end. For cash_2009_HumanK: lines 56-163 of 202; previous article's references at line 27; next article at 164 ("Crystal Structure of the Nuclear Export Receptor CRM1 ...").
- `biblio quotes check` warns (`[WARN] L24 is outside the article (lines 56-163) ...`) when a quote matches outside the cited article; the exit code is unchanged.
- `biblio fulltext --survey [--pools] [--format tsv]` lists such texts.

Survey (2026-11-10), project and pool texts reachable from lit-studio and pixecog: reprints cash_2009_HumanK, leutgeb_2005_IndependentCodes, bringuier_1999_HorizontalPropagation, renart_2010_AsynchronousState, wilson_1994_ReactivationHippocampal; and garcia-cortadella_2021 (a thesis bundling articles), axmacherIntracranialEEGGuide2023 and okeefe_1978 (books whose chapters read as articles). Cell STAR Methods and a paper's own appended Supplemental Information are not flagged (checked on the pixecog pool, 347 texts). Tests in `tests/test_article_span.py`.
