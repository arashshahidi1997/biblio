## Problem

One paper, two citekeys across pools: Ji & Wilson 2007, Nat Neurosci, "Coordinated memory replay in the visual cortex
and hippocampus during sleep". The pixecog pool holds it as `ji_2006_CoordinatedMemory`, lit-studio's bib as
`ji_2007_CoordinatedMemory`. lit-studio's pages now cite only ji_2007 (user decision 2026-11-04), but its dated records
and pixecog still use ji_2006. Lint, locate and quote checks treat them as two papers.

## Evidence

- pixecog pool: `/storage2/arash/projects/pixecog/bib/derivatives/docling/ji_2006_CoordinatedMemory/`
- lit-studio: `bib/srcbib/zotero.bib:1535`, `.projio/biblio/merged.bib:902` (ji_2007)
- lit-studio commit 6bb70406: six wiki pages moved from ji_2006 to ji_2007; `check_page.py` clean before and after.

## Request

A citekey alias (or normalisation across pools) so either key resolves to one record, and lint, `locate` and the
quote checks treat them as one paper. Whether pixecog also renames its pool key is pixecog's call; say so in the reply
if biblio's answer needs it.

## How lit-studio will use it

Older records citing ji_2006 keep resolving; new pages cite ji_2007 only.
